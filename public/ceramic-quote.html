Clear expectations. A coating plan that fits your car.

How much does ceramic coating cost?

For sedans, single-step correction with coating starts at $1,000; two-step correction with coating starts at $1,400. Add $400 for SUVs. Trucks, vans, and other vehicles receive a custom quote. I’ll confirm the final price after reviewing your paint.

Single-step or two-step correction?

Single-step polishing refines gloss and reduces lighter marks. Two-step correction uses a correction stage followed by a finishing polish for paint that needs more work. Choose “Help me choose” in the quiz for a personal recommendation. Results depend on paint condition; deeper scratches may remain.

Is it worth coating a new car?

A new car is a great opportunity to protect a finish you love. New paint can still have delivery contamination or dealer wash marks, so I’ll assess it before recommending preparation.

How long will the coating last?

Longevity depends on the coating selected, preparation, exposure, and maintenance. I’ll explain the product, expected lifespan, curing requirements, and care instructions for your quote.

Does it prevent scratches and rock chips?

A coating helps protect the surface from contamination and makes washing easier. It is not scratch-proof or a shield against rock chips. Careful washing still matters.

Can this be done at my location?

We’ll confirm a suitable workspace and weather conditions first. Ceramic application and curing need the right environment; I’ll discuss the setup and timing with you before booking.
