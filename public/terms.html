Quotes and pricing

Prices on the website and in online quotes are estimates based on your vehicle size and what you tell us. The final price can change if the vehicle's condition is different from what was described, for example heavy pet hair, excessive dirt or sand, mold, biohazards, or paint that needs more correction than expected. If the job needs more than the estimate, we'll explain why and get your agreement before doing the extra work. Sales tax is added to the final invoice where it applies.

Booking, deposits, and cancellations

Online bookings require a $50 deposit, which is applied to the cost of your service. Returning customers may be able to book without a deposit. A booking is a request until we confirm it, and we'll email you when we do.

  • Before we confirm your booking, you can cancel for a full refund of the deposit.
  • After we confirm, you can cancel for a refund if you cancel at least 96 hours (4 days) before the appointment.
  • If you cancel within 96 hours of the appointment, or aren't available when we arrive, the deposit is nonrefundable.
  • An approved reschedule carries your deposit forward, but it doesn't reset a cancellation window that has already passed.
  • If we have to cancel, you can choose a refund or a new time.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days.

Your part

  • Have the vehicle and keys available at the address and time you booked, and make sure we're allowed to work there (for example, under apartment or HOA rules).
  • Remove valuables and personal items before we arrive. We aren't responsible for items left in the vehicle.
  • For most services we need access to an outdoor electrical outlet and a water spigot. Tell us ahead of time if either won't be available.
  • Tell us about anything we should know, such as aftermarket parts or wraps, recent paint work, loose trim, or electrical problems.

Weather and safety

Mobile detailing depends on the weather. If rain, freezing temperatures, high wind, or unsafe conditions would affect the work, we'll contact you to reschedule. Weather-related rescheduling never costs you your deposit.

Results and existing damage

Detailing makes a vehicle cleaner and better protected, but it can't fix everything. Deep scratches, rock chips, failing clear coat, permanent stains, sun damage, and worn or torn materials may improve only partly or not at all. Cleaning can also reveal damage that dirt was hiding. We'll point out existing damage we notice before starting, and we may photograph the vehicle for our records.

Ceramic coatings, paint correction, and similar services carry their own care instructions, which we'll give you at the time of service. Following them is part of getting the expected results.

If something isn't right

Please look over your vehicle when we finish. If we missed something, tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back to fix it at no charge. If you believe we damaged your vehicle, tell us within 48 hours, before any other work is done on it, so we can see it and make it right.

Payment

The remaining balance is due when the job is finished. We accept cards through our secure Stripe checkout, cash, checks, Venmo, and Cash App. A returned check must be paid again by another method. Unpaid balances may be referred for collection.
