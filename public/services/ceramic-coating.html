The preparation behind a lasting finish

Coating and correction do different jobs. Paint correction and polishing refine the finish; ceramic coating helps protect it and simplifies maintenance. A coating does not remove swirls already in the paint.

  1. Review the paint. Discuss condition, existing protection, your goals, and a suitable workspace.
  2. Wash and decontaminate. Remove grime and bonded contamination before polishing.
  3. Correct and refine. Match single-step or two-step correction to the paint rather than promising every scratch will disappear.
  4. Apply and care for the coating. Prepare the surface, apply the coating, and explain curing and maintenance before you leave.

Ceramic coating questions, answered

Is ceramic coating worth it for a new car?

It can be a good fit if you want to protect a finish you already love and make maintenance easier. New paint can still have transport contamination or dealer wash marks, so it should be assessed before coating. You can also compare our new-car decontamination and protection service.

Do I need paint correction before coating?

Preparation matters because coating does not erase swirls or haze. Our coating packages include correction, and we’ll discuss how much your paint needs. Single-step polishing targets gloss and lighter marks; two-step correction includes a correction stage and finishing polish.

How long does ceramic coating last?

It depends on the coating selected, preparation, exposure, and maintenance. I’ll explain the specific coating, expected lifespan, curing requirements, and care instructions when confirming your quote.

Will ceramic coating prevent scratches or rock chips?

It is not scratch-proof and does not stop rock chips. Its strengths are surface protection, gloss, and easier washing. Safe wash techniques still matter, and deep paint damage may need a different repair.

How do I look after a coated car?

Follow the curing instructions provided for your coating, then keep up with gentle washing and drying. Avoid abrasive brush washes and remove bird droppings or bugs promptly. I’ll explain any maintenance recommendations for the product we choose.

Can you apply it at my home?

We’ll confirm whether your location provides suitable conditions for preparation, application, and curing. The right environment matters; the quote conversation is where we settle the workspace and timing.
