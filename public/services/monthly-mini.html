Monthly Mini - Recurring Detail

Price: $100 sedan / $140 SUV, truck, or vanTypical time: 60-75 minutesRequires a full detail in the last 60 days

Who can book: Monthly Mini is only for vehicles that had a full detail with us within the last 60 days. That first full detail sets the standard, and monthly visits keep it there. If it has been longer than 60 days, start with The Works full detail and you can move to Monthly Mini after that.

What This Service Covers

Monthly Mini is a maintenance-focused detail for Asheville-area vehicles that had a full detail with us in the last 60 days. It includes an exterior wash, wheel and tire cleaning, glass cleaning, light interior vacuuming, interior wipe-down, and a SiO2 spray sealant refresh. It is meant to keep a professionally detailed vehicle in clean condition without repeating deep-correction labor on every visit.

When You Might Need It

This service is meant to start within 60 days of a full detail, when you want to maintain consistent results month to month, especially if the vehicle is a daily driver or regularly exposed to pollen, rain, or mountain road dust. A predictable monthly cadence is useful for busy households because the car stays presentable year-round instead of needing occasional emergency cleanups before important events or trips.

Why It Is Worth Doing

Regular monthly detailing reduces long-term wear by preventing contamination layers from setting into clear coat, trim, carpets, and touch surfaces, which protects both appearance and value. It also keeps protective products active and helps avoid larger corrective appointments, making Monthly Mini one of the most practical ways to control detailing costs while enjoying a consistently clean vehicle.

Our Process

  1. Maintenance Wash: Careful hand wash to maintain swirl-free paint.
  2. Wheel Face Clean: Regular removal of corrosive brake dust.
  3. Interior Touch-Up: Light vacuum and dusting to keep the cabin fresh.
  4. Top-Up Protection: Boost existing wax/coating with a spray detailer.

Common Questions

Can I book this as a first-time client?
No. Monthly Mini is only for vehicles that had a full detail with us within the last 60 days. Book a full detail first, and you can switch to Monthly Mini from there.

What if it has been more than 60 days?
Then the car needs a full detail to reset it before Monthly Mini pricing applies. The same goes if the vehicle has gotten very dirty between visits.
