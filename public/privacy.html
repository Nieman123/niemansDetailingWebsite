Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026. Nieman's Detailing (“we,” “us”) is a mobile auto detailing business serving the Asheville, North Carolina area. This policy explains what information we collect through niemansdetailing.com and when you book or pay for a detail, how we use it, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. We don't sell your personal information.

What we collect

When you request a quote

Your name, mobile number, ZIP code, vehicle type and details, the service and add-ons you choose, and any notes you write. We also record which link or ad brought you to the site (for example, a Google ad campaign), how far you got through the quote form, and the IP address the request came from, which helps us spot spam.

When you book an appointment

Your name, mobile number, email address, service address and ZIP code, vehicle, any notes you leave for us (such as a gate code or where to park), the appointment you choose, and your agreement to the deposit and cancellation policy. After the job we keep the invoice, work notes, and photos of the vehicle taken for our records.

When you pay

Card payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's secure checkout page. We never see or store your full card number. We receive a record of the payment, such as the amount, date, and the last digits of the card, and we keep records of cash, check, Venmo, and Cash App payments you make in person.

When you sign in

If you create an account to see your appointments, we store your email address and an account ID from Firebase Authentication (or from Google, if you sign in with Google).

When you browse the site

Like most websites, we use Google Analytics and Google Ads, which use cookies to measure visits, see which pages and ads are working, and show our ads to people who have visited before. If a page on our site hits a technical error, your browser may send us an anonymous error report (the error message and the page address, without personal details) so we can fix it.

How we use it

  • To give you a price, schedule and perform your detail, and contact you about it by phone, text, or email.
  • To send appointment emails: confirmations, receipts, a reminder the day before, and, after a finished job, one request to leave a review.
  • To take deposits and payments, issue refunds, keep tax and accounting records, and prevent fraud and spam.
  • To understand how people find and use the site and improve our ads and service.

We don't add you to a marketing list. If we ever start a newsletter or promotional texts, we'll ask first.

Who helps us handle your information

We use a small number of service providers, who only process your information to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud host the website and store quote requests, appointments, accounts, and job photos.
  • Stripe processes card payments, deposits, and refunds.
  • Resend delivers our appointment emails.
  • Google Calendar holds our work schedule, including your appointment, address, and contact details.
  • Google Maps suggests addresses as you type in the booking form. What you type is sent to Google to find matches.
  • Telegram delivers private notifications to the owner's phone when a quote request or booking comes in.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads measure site visits and ad performance.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or with a buyer if the business is ever sold.

Cookies and ads

Google may use cookies to show you our ads on other websites after you visit ours. You can control this in Google's Ad Settings, opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or block cookies in your browser. The site still works without them.

How long we keep it

We keep appointment, invoice, and payment records for as long as we need them for tax, accounting, and warranty purposes. Quote requests that don't turn into a job are kept so we can follow up, and we'll delete them on request. Technical error logs are deleted after 30 days.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing nieman@niemansdetailing.com or texting (828) 273-3894. We may need to keep some records, such as payment and tax records, where the law requires it. You can also ask us to stop sending appointment reminders or review requests.

Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections, and access limited to the owner's accounts. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will let you know if a breach affects you.

Children

Our services are for adults who own or are responsible for a vehicle. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. Significant changes that affect how we use information you've already given us will be emailed to customers with an account or upcoming appointment.
