# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Vehicle owners in Asheville and surrounding Western North Carolina who want professional mobile detailing without disrupting their day. Many arrive from paid mobile traffic, use the site one-handed outdoors, and may have limited patience, reduced vision, or limited familiarity with multi-step web forms. Their immediate job is to understand the likely price, choose the right service, and request scheduling help with minimal effort.

## Product Purpose

Nieman's Detailing builds trust in a local owner-operated mobile detailing service and turns qualified visitors into quote requests. Success means a visitor can confidently understand the offer, complete the four-step quote flow, and receive a clear price or consultation path without confusion, hidden costs, or unnecessary data entry.

## Brand Personality

Meticulous, direct, and neighborly. The experience should feel like dealing with a skilled local professional who respects the customer's vehicle and time. It should create confidence, ease, and a sense of showroom-ready craft.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic tunnel-wash branding, luxury posturing, aggressive urgency, auto-industry clutter, low-contrast dark-on-dark controls, tiny labels, decorative effects that obscure the task, and long forms that ask for information before showing value.

## Design Principles

1. Put the fastest path to a credible price first.
2. Make every primary action obvious and reachable with one thumb.
3. Earn trust through transparent pricing, plain language, and local proof.
4. Preserve momentum across the flow and make recovery effortless.
5. Let detailing craftsmanship show through precision, not visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard and screen-reader navigation, 48-by-48-pixel minimum touch targets, visible focus states, reduced motion, high text contrast, 16-pixel minimum body and input text on mobile, clear inline errors, semantic progress announcements, and layouts that remain usable at 200% zoom and narrow mobile widths.
